Night sky

Point the phone and the catalog is drawn on the sky from where you are, what time it is, and which way the lens faces. Polaris is true north. Its height above the horizon is your latitude. A photo can snap those labels onto real stars when the pattern is clear.

Choosing a photo from the camera roll fills the time and the GPS fix when the file has them. This browser can also fill latitude and longitude from the device location. A camera clock has no time zone, so that time is read as UTC. Each star-like point is named on its own. The picture is darkened so those points stand out from the sky.
